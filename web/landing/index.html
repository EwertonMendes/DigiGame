DESIGN DIRECTION

Preparation shapes the team. Tactics decide the battle.

01

Branching evolution

Digivolve and degenerate through different routes in the DigiLab. Returning to level 1 is intended to be a strategic choice.

FOUNDATION IMPLEMENTED
02

Readable tactics

Movement, range, attack shapes, terrain, displacement, and turn order create decisions before an attack lands.

IMPLEMENTED PROTOTYPE
03

Team synergy

A planned Link system will let nearby allies coordinate special actions. Teams are limited to three to keep every member meaningful.

PLANNED
04

A growing home

The campaign hub is designed to expand visually and functionally as the story and community progress.

EARLY / EXPERIMENTAL

WHAT EXISTS NOW

A real prototype, with an honest boundary around it.

Hub, combat, progression foundations, and Web support are running. The campaign, Link system, final content, balance, and player onboarding are not complete.

THE FIRST PUBLIC BUILD OPENS WHEN

The vertical slice can stand on its own.

  • A coherent 20–30 minute hub-to-reward loop works.
  • One tactical decision and one evolution route are meaningful.
  • Desktop and Android browser sessions have no blockers.
  • At least 70% of the closed-test group finishes the main flow.
